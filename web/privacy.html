Forkast Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Forkast is a personal meal planner, shopping list and recipe box. It's a website with no server of its own: everything runs in your browser.

What Forkast stores, and where

  • On your device. Your recipes, recipe photos, weekly plans and shopping lists are saved in your browser's storage on each device you use.
  • In your Google Drive, if you connect it. Forkast keeps a copy in a folder named "Forkast" in your own Google Drive, so your devices can share it. Nobody else receives this data, including the people who made Forkast.

Google account access

When you connect Google Drive, Forkast asks for:

  • Access to files it creates in your Drive (drive.file). Forkast can read and change only the files it made itself. It can't see any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address, which is shown on the "Your data" screen so you know which account is connected.

The sign-in token stays in your browser and expires after about an hour. Forkast doesn't sell, share or transfer your Google data, and doesn't use it for advertising. Its use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, open Forkast, tap ⋮, then "Disconnect". To remove Forkast's access completely, go to your Google Account permissions. Your files stay in Drive until you delete them.

Other services the app contacts

  • TheMealDB (themealdb.com) receives what you type in the Discover search box.
  • Jina AI's reader (r.jina.ai) receives the web links you ask Forkast to read, and opens those pages to return the recipe. A recipe's photo is then shown from the website it came from.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the app's fonts and its photo text reader.

Recipe photos are read on your device. The images aren't sent to anyone for reading.

Forkast has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
